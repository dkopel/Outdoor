# Food Storage in Bear Country

## Why it matters more than you think
A bear that gets human food once is a problem; a bear that gets it twice is often killed by wildlife managers. "A fed bear is a dead bear" is literally true. Beyond the conservation issue, an unprotected camp invites bears, raccoons, mice, and other animals that destroy gear chewing through it to reach scented items. Treat everything with scent — food, toothpaste, sunscreen, lip balm, trash, dirty cooking pots — as bait, and store it accordingly.

## Bear canisters
Hard-sided bear canisters are the gold standard in most U.S. bear country and are required in many areas (Sierra Nevada, Adirondacks, Olympic, Grand Teton coastal areas, etc.). Approved canisters are crush- and bite-resistant. Place a canister at least 100 yards (90 m) from your sleeping area and 100 yards from food prep. Do not tie it to a tree or rock — a curious bear can roll it off a cliff into water. Place it in flat ground away from drop-offs and water.

## Bear hangs
A proper bear hang is harder than it looks. Target: food bag 12 feet (3.6 m) off the ground, 6 feet (1.8 m) below the branch it hangs from, and 6 feet from the trunk. Use 50 feet of cord with a small rock-bag for throwing. The PCT method (counterbalance) and the "two-rope" method are both more secure than a single line over a branch, which a black bear's cub can shimmy out to. Bear hangs do not work for grizzlies — they pull down the tree or break the limb. Use a canister in grizzly country.

## Ursack and soft-sided alternatives
Ursacks (kevlar food bags) are approved for some areas but not all — check current regulations for the park or forest. They depend on the bear giving up before chewing through, which most do but not all. Always combine with an odor-proof liner. Soft food bags hung from a branch with no other protection are not adequate in bear country.

## Cooking and eating
Cook and eat at least 100 yards (90 m) downwind from where you sleep. Never bring food, food wrappers, or scented items into your tent. Strain dishwater and pack it out where it makes sense, or scatter it widely 100+ yards from camp. Burn off food residue from cooking pots before storing. Change out of cook-stained clothes before bed and store them with food.
